# Boarding Party: cargo cover and shootable airlocks

Drake approved this follow-up on 2026-11-06. It supersedes the Life Support cover presentation and button/Q/E airlock controls in earlier design/playtest records. Refs #72, #39. Engineering completion does not establish owner or device acceptance.

## Current behavior

- Six fixed cargo stacks (one per side at three depths) sit on the floor. Each has a smaller case resting on a larger case, perspective top/side faces, straps, seams, labels and contact shadows. Cargo and boarders share projected depth ordering. Covered enemies duck behind their own stack; only a helmet peeks above it. Exposed aiming regions and rule-owned projection/hit radii retain their existing dimensions.
- At most two boarders are active, one slot per side. A freed slot can respawn at the far depth on the existing spawn cadence; attack timings, depth progression and rifle cooldowns remain unchanged.
- Enemy state words, door names/prompts and recharge text are removed. After five seconds the opening guidance disappears too. Ducking, raised rifles, muzzle lights, motion and lasers communicate state. An inset wall control lights during a crossing, with eight small recharge segments below it. The external main HUD remains.
- Each side door has a rule-owned target at (100,280) or (380,280), radius 23 logical pixels. A target lights during that side's relocating boarder approach/occupancy (the first two thirds of the existing 1.6-second relocation) when its independent airlock is ready. Departure, absence of a crossing, opening and recharge leave the target unlit. These dimensions are provisional playtest tuning.
- Shoot a glowing target with the rifle to activate its door. Touch aims plus separate Fire; mouse aims and primary click/hold fires; keyboard arrows/WASD aim at the existing normalized 225px/s cap and Space/Enter fires. Fire also works with mouse or keyboard aim. There are no Vent buttons or Q/E shortcuts. Controls use the same rifle cooldown as enemy shots. A shot hits one lit control or one exposed boarder, with control priority if their aiming areas overlap; an unlit control does not intercept a shot.
- Airlocks retain a 0.6-second warning and independent eight-second recharge (16 seconds on Natural 1). Occupancy is evaluated at warning expiry: a late shot may open an empty bay and still consumes the recharge. Ejection wins a tied enemy emission; already-fired lasers remain live.
- The revised generated corridor contains recessed wall airlocks with matching perspective, metal, wear, shadows and amber practical lighting. The original generated door leaves are sampled directly from that same background texture, split into two cropped images per side and clipped by two reusable geometry masks. During opening they slide behind the jambs without scaling/compressing the panels. No full-door luminous outline is added. Missing artwork uses shaded perspective polygons. See [artwork provenance](illustrated-artwork.md) and the [exact two editing prompts](boarding-corridor-art-prompts.json).
- Doors slide open, airflow streaks move into the opening, and ejected boarders move toward the doorway while shrinking/fading for approximately 0.45 seconds. This duration is provisional. Ejection and its 125-point reward occur once at warning expiry; animation has no collision/damage/scoring role. Reduced motion suppresses decorative streak motion and spin. Simulation timers freeze on pause and clear on retry. Pooled graphics/images are reused and hidden outside this mode.

## Covered-helmet review fix — 2026-11-06

The [P2 review finding](https://github.com/drakejrobert-sudo/galaxy-grown-games/pull/75#discussion_r4498815066) remained valid after floating labels were removed: the warning helmet was wholly behind the upper case. Its center now sits at `b.y + 2 × scale` rather than `b.y + 15 × scale`, retaining the nine-scaled-pixel radius and lower rendering depth. The upper cap and a narrow amber visor clear the case silhouette while the lower helmet/body remain hidden. No labels, hitboxes, mechanics, timings, scoring or assets change.

The new rendering regression failed against the old position and passes with the fix on both sides at all three depths. `npm test` passes all **342** tests, `npm run build` passes with the existing deferred-Phaser advisory, and `git diff --check` passes. The Boarding browser suite also passes its existing controls/lifecycle/map flows and **36 pixel comparisons** (two sides × three depths × desktop/390px/320px × artwork/fallback). Every comparison detects helmet and amber visor pixels above cargo and zero changed body-region pixels beneath the occluding front face. Pixel sampling waits for Phaser's Canvas render rather than reading queued drawing commands. Screenshots for each depth and width were captured under `/tmp/helmet-qa/`; narrow art and fallback screenshots were visually inspected. These are emulated-touch/desktop engineering checks, not actual iPhone/iPad Safari acceptance. The earlier broader original-eight-mode validation remains applicable; this correction is confined to the Boarding Life Support warning drawing.
